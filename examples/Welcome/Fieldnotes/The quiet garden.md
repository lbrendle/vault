# The quiet garden

The best thing about a garden notebook is the permission to pay attention.

This is a fictional observation: seedlings near the eastern window opened their leaves earlier in the morning. That is a pattern worth recording, not yet an explanation.

## What to notice next

- The temperature beside each tray.
- Hours of direct light.
- Watering time and soil moisture.
- Whether the pattern repeats on an overcast day.

See [[Ideas/Good questions]] and [[Projects/Seed library]].

> [!note] Keep the distinction
> “These seedlings opened earlier” is an observation. “Morning light caused it” needs a comparison.
