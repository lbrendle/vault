# Vault demonstration

The release includes a 1080p product tour and a shorter 28-second social cut. Both use real native app captures and a fictional Commonplace library. No personal research, pairing codes, or private conversations appear.

Mac footage includes reading and linked navigation, graph exploration, appearance changes, model setup, and a real native Qwen 3.5 4B response with a working citation. Mobile captures come from Apple’s iOS Simulator; iPad footage includes sidebar collapse, linked reading, and navigation. The video has no music or narration, and the main story is carried by text.

The film is an edited product demonstration, not a latency or sync benchmark. The local-generation segment plays at its recorded speed. The Mac Starter archive includes Qwen 2B; Qwen 4B is an optional in-app download.

Download the videos from [the beta release](https://github.com/lbrendle/vault/releases/tag/v0.5.0-beta.1). The screenshot and demo artwork are project assets under the repository license.
